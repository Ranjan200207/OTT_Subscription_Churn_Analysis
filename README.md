SUMMARY 

Engineered an end-to-end churn analytics pipeline for an OTT 
subscription dataset (20+ KPIs), segmented the customer 
base into risk tiers using composite churn scores, exposed a 
significant lifetime value gap between churned and retained 
cohorts, and recommended prioritising Premium annual-plan 
retention over Basic monthly acquisition to maximise long
term revenue yield. Identified a 28.6% overall churn rate. The 
retention rate is around 71.4%.

CHURN DEFINITION BY BUISNESS 

Churn analysis is the process of figuring out why customers 
stop doing business with you. For this, we only need to look at 
three things:  
• "Who": Identifying which customers left or will leave in 
future  
• "Why": Analyzing their behavior before they left.  
• "When": Finding the "danger zone" – point when a user leave
